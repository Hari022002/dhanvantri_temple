Welcome to the official website of Dhanvantri Temple, Hosur.
Our temple is dedicated to Lord Dhanvantri, the divine healer and incarnation of Lord Vishnu, worshipped for health, healing, and holistic well-being. Located in the spiritually vibrant town of Hosur, the temple offers a serene atmosphere for devotees seeking divine grace and inner peace. This online platform serves as a bridge between the temple and devotees worldwide, enabling participation in poojas, donations, event registrations, and staying connected with temple activities.
